# Annotated source registry — Decisioning Twin V3.1

Reviewed November 3, 2026.

Original frameworks and synthetic scenarios are explicitly separate from regulatory evidence and research findings.

## [01] Galbraith, J. R. (1974) — Organization Design: An Information Processing View

- Category: FOUNDATION
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1287/inte.4.3.28
- Why cited: Explains organizations as information-processing arrangements responding to task uncertainty and cognitive limits.
- Evidence boundary: Classical organizational theory, not an AI-era dealer experiment.

## [02] Coase, R. H. (1937) — The Nature of the Firm

- Category: FOUNDATION
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0335.1937.tb00002.x
- Why cited: Provides the economic account of why coordination costs influence organizational boundaries.
- Evidence boundary: Useful for framing hypotheses; does not establish how AI will reorganize dealers.

## [03] Vaccaro, M., Almaatouq, A., & Malone, T. (2024) — When combinations of humans and AI are useful: A systematic review and meta-analysis

- Category: EMPIRICAL RESEARCH
- Link: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-02024-1
- Why cited: Analyzes 106 experiments; human–AI teams did not consistently outperform the stronger standalone performer.
- Evidence boundary: Studies from 2020–mid-2023; not a Canadian dealer sample.

## [04] Kim, E. M., Garg, A., Peng, K., & Garg, N. (2025) — Correlated Errors in Large Language Models

- Category: EMPIRICAL RESEARCH
- Link: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/kim25e.html
- Why cited: Shows that errors can be substantially correlated across large language models.
- Evidence boundary: General LLM evaluation, not a measured dealer supervision failure.

## [05] CSA & CIRO (2025) — Joint Staff Notice 31-368: KYC, KYP and Suitability Determination Practices

- Category: CANADIAN REGULATORY
- Link: https://www.ciro.ca/newsroom/publications/joint-canadian-securities-administrators-canadian-investment-regulatory-organization-staff-notice-31-0
- Why cited: Details obligations and review findings on KYC, KYP, suitable actions, reasonable alternatives, concentration, liquidity and reassessment.
- Evidence boundary: Applies according to registration, dealer business model and relevant rules.

## [06] CIRO (2026) — Compliance Report for 2026: Helping Dealers with Compliance

- Category: CANADIAN REGULATORY
- Link: https://www.ciro.ca/newsroom/publications/ciro-compliance-report-2026-helping-dealers-compliance
- Why cited: States examination interest in dealer AI usage and operational controls and identifies possible material-change notification considerations.
- Evidence boundary: Not approval of this simulation or a universal prohibition on dealer AI.

## [07] NIST (2023) — Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage

- Category: AI GOVERNANCE
- Link: https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/
- Why cited: Provides voluntary structures for organizational AI governance, measurement and risk management.
- Evidence boundary: Guidance framework, not Canadian securities law.

## [08] NIST NCCoE (2026) — Identity and Authority of Software Agents — Concept Paper

- Category: AI GOVERNANCE
- Link: https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/02/new-concept-paper-identity-and-authority-software-agents
- Why cited: Highlights authorization, audit and identity considerations when AI agents access data, tools and applications.
- Evidence boundary: A concept paper, not a finalized security standard or Canadian regulatory requirement.

## [09] Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (2023) — Principles for responsible, trustworthy and privacy-protective generative AI

- Category: CANADIAN PRIVACY
- Link: https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/technology/artificial-intelligence/gd_principles_ai/
- Why cited: Identifies privacy, accuracy and governance considerations, including treating inferences about identifiable individuals as personal information.
- Evidence boundary: Joint guidance principles; specific legal obligations depend on applicable law and circumstances.

## [10] Bank of England & FCA (2026) — Artificial intelligence in UK financial services — 2026

- Category: SECTOR CONTEXT
- Link: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/report/2026/artificial-intelligence-in-uk-financial-services-2026
- Why cited: Shows expanding AI use across surveyed UK financial services; illustrates diffusion without proving improved decision quality.
- Evidence boundary: UK survey respondents, not Canadian wealth management dealer adoption rates.

## [11] CIRO (2026) — Client Focused Reforms (CFRs) — Dealer Guidance Hub

- Category: CANADIAN REGULATORY
- Link: https://www.ciro.ca/firms/current-members/dealer-compliance/client-focused-reforms-cfrs
- Why cited: Consolidates practical bulletins and guidance on conflicts, KYC, product due diligence, KYP and suitability.
- Evidence boundary: Read together with applicable rules; guidance is not a substitute for them.
