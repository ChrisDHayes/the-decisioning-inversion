## Evidence boundaries

- All simulated decisions, faults, actions, risk flags, permissions, evidence statuses and outcomes are **synthetic, deterministic teaching cases**.
- The sources support the surrounding theoretical, regulatory and empirical arguments; **they do not validate the specific simulated decision outcomes**.
- The five conceptual diagrams are explanations, not observed financial results. The GitHub SVG web renditions preserve the propositions but are distinct from the original master graphics in the downloadable publication ZIP.
- The original formatted research DOCX/PDF, original Substack DOCX/PDF, and high-resolution PNG masters remain in the downloadable publication package. The current GitHub connector supports text/SVG publishing; those binary originals have not yet been transferred to the repository.
- This is not legal, regulatory, compliance or investment advice, nor a ready-to-use dealer control system.
